# Code of conduct

Short, because it needs to be read.

## The rule

**Argue with the work, not the person.**

Telling us our calibration is overstated, our ledger denominator is self-serving, or our Malay
wording is clumsy is not rudeness — it is the reason this repository exists, and you do not need to
soften it. Telling someone they are stupid for having built it that way is a different thing, and it
is not welcome here.

## Specifically

Not acceptable, in issues, pull requests or email:

- personal attacks, insults, or comments about someone's nationality, religion, race, gender,
  sexuality, disability or age;
- harassment of any kind, including unwanted contact after being asked to stop;
- posting anyone's private information;
- sexualised language or imagery;
- deliberately derailing a thread, or repeating a point that has been answered in order to exhaust
  the other person.

Also not acceptable here, for reasons specific to what we do:

- promoting betting operators, tipping services, signal groups or paid picks;
- asking contributors or maintainers what to pick, or pressing after being told no;
- any suggestion that a reader should risk money on anything we publish.

Those get removed on sight and are not treated as a matter of opinion.

## Scope

This applies in this repository's issues and discussions, and in direct contact that arises from
them.

## Reporting

Email **1qazxsw2sky5@gmail.com**. Reports are read by a person, not a bot, and are not shared beyond
the people who need to act on them. You will get a reply.

If the problem is with one of us, say so — that report goes to the founder directly and is handled
the same way.

## What happens

In order, depending on severity: a private word, a public correction in the thread, removal of the
content, a temporary block, a permanent block. Serious harassment skips to the end.

We will not use this document to shut down criticism of the product. If you ever think we have, say
so publicly — that will be a fair thing to hold us to.
